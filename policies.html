Customer Policies

Important: These are starter policy templates. Review and adapt them to your actual business practices before publishing.

Shipping & Delivery

We deliver across India where service is available. Shipping charges, dispatch estimates, courier method and delivery timelines are confirmed at the time of order. Free shipping applies only when explicitly offered for a product or order. Customers should provide a complete address and reachable phone number.

Returns, Refunds & Cancellation

Before payment, contact Alfa Gems World to confirm the exact item, weight, dimensions, product description, price basis, certification details and return eligibility. The seller should provide the applicable return, cancellation, replacement and refund terms in writing before accepting payment. Do not publish this template as a final policy until the actual terms have been reviewed.

Privacy

Customer details should be used only for responding to enquiries, processing orders, delivery, support and legal obligations. A live website must publish a complete privacy notice describing data collected, purposes, processors, retention, security measures and customer rights. This demo does not send customer data to a server.

Terms & Conditions

Product photographs, descriptions, gemstone origin, treatment and certification must accurately describe the specific item. Confirm availability and final total before payment. This demo is a storefront prototype and does not itself process payments or create confirmed orders.
